# Working on The Prosperity Gap

The user is a policy writer, not a developer. This page is his personal project
(no institutional branding) and shares its look with seanspeer.com and
Back of the Envelope (seanspeer.com/tax): same tokens, fonts, site bar and
slim navy masthead.

- One focal point: the headline share ("74¢ for every dollar"). Keep it the
  loudest thing on the page.
- Every number on the page must come from `docs/data.json`, built by `build.py`.
  Never type a figure into the HTML.
- Keep the method candid. If you add a measure or a comparison, add what can
  distort it to "What can move the numbers".
- Canada vs the US only (the user's decision); GDP per person is the headline measure.
- After changing `build.py`, run it and check the printed summary and the sanity checks.
